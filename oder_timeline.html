Implementation Notes

Flexibility & Adaptation

  • Data-dependent timing: If only 2025 catch log data exist, Paper 1 becomes cross-sectional baseline (submitted Y1Q2-Q3), Paper 3 deferred to Year 2 to allow 2026 data collection for temporal analysis
  • Polish partnerships: Cross-border surveys and workshops contingent on partnership development. German-side deployment proceeds regardless; Polish component added when feasible
  • Survey sequencing: Resident ES survey prioritized if budget constrained; creel module deployed as resources permit

Resource Allocation

  • Year 1: 60% analysis (Papers 1-3), 30% survey development, 10% stakeholder mapping
  • Year 2: 40% survey deployment/analysis, 30% writing (Papers 1-3 revisions), 30% stakeholder engagement
  • Year 3: 50% integration papers, 30% policy/dissemination, 20% project synthesis

Dependencies & Critical Path

  • Integration papers require completed survey deployment (Year 2 Q1+)
  • Workshop 2 requires survey results available for presentation
  • Paper 3 requires either multi-year catch log data OR reframing as cross-sectional
  • Cross-border comparison requires Polish survey deployment (feasibility TBD)

Conference Targets

  • Year 1 Q3: European Inland Fisheries and Aquaculture Advisory Commission (EIFAAC)
  • Year 2 Q2: American Fisheries Society (AFS) Annual Meeting
  • Year 3 Q2: International Institute of Fisheries Economics & Trade (IIFET)

Publication Pipeline Overview

The project targets 5-7 peer-reviewed publications across three tiers:

Tier 1: Catch Log Papers (Years 1-2)

  1. Recovery trajectory (NAJFM / Fisheries Research)
  2. Motivation & specialization (Human Dimensions of Wildlife / Leisure Sciences)
  3. Harvest behavior (Fisheries Management & Ecology / CJFAS)

Tier 2: Integration Papers (Years 2-3)

  1. Stated vs. revealed preferences (Ecological Economics / Ecosystem Services)
  2. Cross-border ES comparison - if Polish data available (Environmental Management / Journal of Environmental Management)

Tier 3: Synthesis & Methods (Year 3)

  1. Transboundary governance lessons (potentially co-authored with Polish partners)
  2. Methodological paper on integrating catch logs with stated preference surveys
