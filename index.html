Max Birdsong

PhD · Postdoctoral Researcher · IFB Potsdam

I'm a human dimensions researcher at the Leibniz Institute of Freshwater Ecology and Inland Fisheries (IFB Potsdam), working on the OderTogether project — a cross-border social-ecological study of the Oder River following the 2022 ecological disaster. My work combines resident surveys, citizen science data, and spatial analysis to understand how communities relate to rivers and how those relationships are affected by environmental disruption. This site documents the methods and analyses behind that work, alongside earlier projects in fisheries social science, wildlife management, and recreational behavior.

Wolf Survey — End-to-End Data Pipeline

Turning three waves of public opinion into one clear story

Over a decade, Montana surveyed residents about wolves—tolerance, hunting, trust, and impacts. By 2023, the instruments and files didn't line up: different schemas, overlapping IDs, shifting labels. This project rebuilt the data into a single, comparable record and delivered weighted, presentation-ready trends.

Approach: co-design the latest wave for comparability → harmonize legacy files into a canonical layout → fix duplicates and standardize fields in SQL → apply weights and generate faceted charts and a briefing deck in R.

Outcome: a reproducible pipeline with code-backed visuals showing how tolerance for wolves and tolerance for wolf hunting moved across groups and time.

Survey design & implementation

The 2023 questionnaire was co-designed to match 2012/2017 on the things that matter—strata, weights, and key constructs—so trends compare cleanly.
