🎤 INTERVIEW KEY - ES Measurement

  • 5-point importance scales (optimal scale length per Revilla et al. 2014—cited in Pope et al. 2026)
  • 14 ES items cover provisioning, regulating, cultural, supporting services (MEA framework)
  • Higher numbers = more important (consistent direction across all questions per Vaske et al. 2026)

C

Priority & Tradeoff

🎤 INTERVIEW KEY - Tradeoff Methods

  • Addresses ceiling effects: Importance ratings often show everything is "very important" (literature gap identified in review)
  • Top 3 ranking: Forces explicit prioritization—reveals relative preferences when resources are limited
  • Budget allocation (100 points): Captures preference intensity and tradeoffs simultaneously. Based on discrete choice / conjoint analysis principles
  • Why two methods? Top 3 = ordinal preferences (easier, less cognitive burden). Budget = ratio-level data (preference strength). Convergent validity check
  • Management relevance: Decision-makers face real budget constraints—this mirrors actual allocation decisions
  • Cross-border insight: Do Germans and Poles prioritize same services when forced to choose? Critical for transboundary governance
