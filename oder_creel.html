Your Fishing Background

🎤 INTERVIEW KEY - Specialization/Centrality

  • We measure CENTRALITY, not full "specialization": Landon et al. (2026) new guidance—if you don't measure all 3 dimensions (behavioral commitment, psychological commitment, skill/cognition), don't call it "specialization." Use the specific dimension name.
  • Centrality = psychological commitment: "How central is fishing to your life/identity?" Validated 3-5 item scales from Kyle et al. (2026) compendium (DOI: 10.18738/T8/LFWX64)
  • Why centrality matters: Predicts conservation-oriented behavior (C&R) during ecological stress (Sutton & Ditton 2001). More centrally-involved anglers are "more sensitive to fishing-related stimuli" and "more likely to engage in protective behaviors" (Landon et al. 2026, p.171)
  • Context-dependent relationships: Specialized anglers under stress may show LOWER satisfaction due to violated expectations (Koemle et al. 2024)—competing hypothesis to test
  • Q12 proxy for centrality: "How important is fishing to you?" captures centrality dimension but validated multi-item scale preferred. Keep brief for on-site interview (<8 min target)
