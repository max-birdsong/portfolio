Catch Log Survey Structure

OderAngeln Project survey schema and question flow

The OderAngeln catch log study uses two different survey instruments depending on permit type. Annual permit holders receive a comprehensive logbook for recording all trips, while day/week permit holders complete a streamlined online survey after each fishing trip.

Annual Permit Survey

Target: Anglers with annual Oder fishing permits

Format: Paper logbook or online catch log

Purpose: Comprehensive season-long documentation

Day/Week Permit Survey

Target: Anglers with day or week permits

Format: Online survey (mobile-friendly)

Purpose: Quick trip documentation (~5 min)

Annual Permit Survey Structure

Comprehensive background questionnaire completed once at registration, followed by per-trip logging throughout the season.

Background Questions (One-time)
  • How many years have you been actively fishing?
  • On how many days do you estimate you went fishing in 2025?
  • Are you a member of a fishing club?
    • Yes, active member
    • Yes, passive member
    • No, not a member
  • Which annual permit did you purchase for 2026?
    • Large Oder map – for members
    • Large Oder map – for unorganized anglers
    • Annual permit for designated sections
  • How did you purchase or obtain your annual pass?
    • Online fishing license shop
    • Issued by Brandenburg Angling Association
    • Through a fishing business
    • Via a fishing shop
    • Online fishing card shop (e.g., Fiskado)
    • Other issuing points
  • Participation preference:
    • Catch logbook in paper format (DIN A5, waterproof)
    • Online fishing log (entries via mobile devices)
Demographics (One-time)
  • When were you born? (year of birth, minimum age 14 years)
  • First name
  • Surname
  • Street, house number
  • Postal code
  • Location
  • Landline telephone number
  • Mobile phone number
  • E-mail
Per-Trip Logging (Repeated)

Completed for each fishing trip throughout the season

  • Date of fishing trip
  • Time started and finished
  • Body of water name
  • Nearest location
  • Total expenses for the trip
  • Target fish species
  • Fishing method:
    • Riverbank or boat
    • Spin or still
    • One rod or two rods
  • Did you catch at least one fish?
  • For each fish caught ≥20cm:
    • Species
    • Length
    • Harvest (yes/no)
  • Satisfaction (1-5 scale)

Day/Week Permit Survey Structure

Streamlined survey completed after each fishing trip. Designed for mobile completion in ~5 minutes.

Page 1: Trip Timing
  • Date of your fishing trip
  • What time did you start?
  • What time did you finish?
Page 2: Location & Expenses
  • Please specify the name of the body of water
  • Please specify the nearest location
  • Please state the total expenses for the trip
Page 3: Fishing Method
  • What is your target fish species?
  • Please specify the fishing method:
    • Riverbank/boat
    • Spin/still
    • One rod or two rods
  • Did you catch at least one fish?
Page 4: Catch Details
  • Fish longer than 20 cm:
    • Species
    • Length
    • Harvest (yes/no)
    • For each individual fish caught
Page 5: Satisfaction
  • Satisfaction (1-5 scale)
Page 6: Fishing Experience
  • How many years have you been actively fishing?
  • How many days did you go fishing in 2024?
Page 7: Club Membership
  • Are you a member of a fishing club?
Page 8: Permit Information
  • Which fishing permit did you purchase?
  • How did you purchase it?
Page 9: Demographics
  • Year of birth
Page 10: Target Species & Equipment
  • List three favorite target species
  • Value of equipment
Page 11: Leisure Activities
  • Number of hobbies
  • How important is fishing as a hobby compared to other leisure activities?
    • 1st most important
    • 2nd most important
    • 3rd most important
    • More than 3rd
Page 12: Fishing Preferences
  • Fishing preferences (scale 1-5: never to exclusively):
    • Spin fishing
    • Fishing the Oder
    • Still fishing
    • Target a specific species

Shared Data Elements

Both survey types collect the following core information for comparability:

  • Trip characteristics: date, time, location, expenses
  • Fishing method: bank/boat, spin/still, number of rods
  • Target species
  • Catch data: species, length (≥20cm), harvest decision
  • Satisfaction rating (1-5)
  • Angler background: years fishing, annual fishing days, club membership
  • Permit type and purchase method
